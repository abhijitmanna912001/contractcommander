FREELANCE SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Freelance Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between StartupCo ("Client") and the undersigned freelance contractor ("Contractor").

1. Services. Contractor shall provide UI/UX design services as described in the attached project brief, on a project basis.

2. Payment. Client shall pay Contractor $5,000 upon completion of the project, payable within thirty (30) days of final delivery and Client's written acceptance of deliverables.

3. Liability. Contractor's total liability under this Agreement shall be limited to the total fees paid under this Agreement. Contractor shall not be liable for indirect or consequential damages.

4. Intellectual Property. Upon full payment, all deliverables created specifically for this project shall be assigned to Client. Contractor retains ownership of any pre-existing design assets, templates, or tools used in creating the deliverables, and grants Client a non-exclusive license to use such pre-existing assets as incorporated into the final deliverables.

5. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement with fourteen (14) days' written notice. If Client terminates without cause, Client shall pay Contractor for all work completed up to the termination date on a pro-rata basis.

6. Confidentiality. Contractor agrees to keep Client's business information confidential during the engagement and for one (1) year following termination.

7. Non-Compete. Contractor agrees not to accept work from Client's direct competitors within the same city for a period of six (6) months following completion of this project.

8. Revisions. Client is entitled to up to three (3) rounds of revisions. Additional revisions beyond this shall be billed at Contractor's standard hourly rate, to be agreed upon in advance.

9. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the state in which Client is headquartered. Disputes shall first be addressed through good-faith negotiation before either party pursues formal legal action.

10. Independent Contractor Status. Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee of Client, and is responsible for their own taxes and benefits.
